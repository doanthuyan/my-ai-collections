# Target Output Format Schema

Ensure the final agent output strictly matches this structure:

## Candidate

Name [Full name]
Education
Certificate

## Skills
List summary technical & soft skills of candidate

## Domains
Bullet list business domain knowledge of candidate

## Projects
Numbered list projects candidate participated.

Structure:
1. [Project name]
Role [Role this candidate played]
Team Size [Number of members]
Business purpose [vision, targets, description]
Scope of work [what areas this candidate participated]
Tools [Techstack]
Responsibilities [Candidate's reponsibility]
